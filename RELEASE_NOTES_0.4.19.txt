Pixel_GO v0.4.19

Changes in this build
- Removed wave-based room pacing in floor combat rooms.
- Room combat now uses continuous escalating pressure with no wave breaks.
- Enemy spawn tempo ramps up during each room as time/progress increases.
- Live enemy cap also ramps up during the room to create mounting pressure.
- Doubled total enemy quota per room (roughly 2x room mob count).
- Boss/final rooms keep pre-boss pressure, then spawn boss once the room quota is exhausted and the room is temporarily clear.

Notes
- HUD wave text is hidden because room flow is no longer wave-based.
- This build is a direct continuation of v0.4.18.
